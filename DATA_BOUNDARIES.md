# Data and asset boundaries

## Included

- manuscript text, bibliography, tables, and publication figures;
- non-identifying aggregate metrics, contrasts, audit CSVs, and plot-source tables;
- candidate-bank locking audits and canonical feature-set summaries that contain no participant identifiers;
- formal locking propositions, proofs, complexity analysis, and implementation crosswalks;
- deterministic figure builders and validation scripts;
- checksums and provenance manifests for nonrestricted analytic starting boundaries.

## Excluded

- participant-level ADNI, AMP-AD, CGGA, TCGA, or VGH matrices and identifiers;
- DICOM or derived images, segmentation masks, clinical linkage files, and QC records;
- participant- or prediction-level records, including TCGA outer-fold predictions;
- provider-controlled workbooks and source archives whose redistribution permission has not been established;
- credentials, access tokens, complete GA run directories, checkpoints, model binaries, and raw predictions;
- local scratch directories, caches, rendered QA pages, and machine-specific paths;
- package source code, which is maintained in the companion software repository.

Access to controlled research data remains governed by the applicable source
provider and institutional procedures. Aggregate materials in this repository do
not grant redistribution rights to the underlying datasets.
