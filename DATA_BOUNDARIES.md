# Data and asset boundaries

## Included

- manuscript text, bibliography, tables, and aggregate figures;
- nonparticipant aggregate audit CSVs and plot-source tables;
- formal regret-locking propositions, proofs, complexity analysis, and implementation crosswalk;
- revision and validation reports;
- scripts that document aggregate analysis and figure-generation procedures.

## Excluded

- participant-level ADNI, AMP-AD, or CGGA matrices and identifiers;
- provider-controlled source files, credentials, access tokens, and private archives;
- complete GA run directories, checkpoints, model binaries, and raw predictions;
- local scratch directories, caches, rendered QA pages, and machine-specific paths;
- package source code, which is maintained in the companion package repository.

Access to controlled research data remains governed by the applicable source-provider procedures. The presence of aggregate results in this repository does not grant redistribution rights to the underlying datasets.
