\textit{Design and interpretation.} The local protocol was frozen before candidate generation; this does not denote registration in an external preregistry. The analysis conditions on one fixed TCGA development partition of 392 participants, with the original preprocessing, screening universes and RFECV targets held fixed. The primary scorer uses candidate-specific RF seeds and common development folds; the secondary scorer fixes the RF seed at 342. The same 240 candidates were reused across scorers and nested $R$ prefixes. Six pairs share four banks, prefixes are correlated, and branches share participants; no confidence intervals or $p$-values are reported. Exact agreement is zero for both policies in every condition. This is a new CPU-backend extension, not reproduction of GPU trajectories or participant-level or external validation. The later 16-bank, five-candidate calibration analysis is a separate post hoc reblocking of these same runs. Full-precision source files contain all 108 pairs, 72 bank selections, 18 condition summaries and six equal-branch means. Displayed values are rounded to six decimals.
