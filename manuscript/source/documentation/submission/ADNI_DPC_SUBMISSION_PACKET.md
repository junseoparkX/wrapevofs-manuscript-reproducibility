## Official upload route

1. Log into the LONI Image and Data Archive (IDA).
2. Select **My Account**.
3. In the ADNI project row, select **Update**.
4. Open the **Publication Update** tab.
5. Enter the publication information and upload the near-final manuscript.
6. Save the portal confirmation, submission date, and any DPC correspondence.

ADNI states that DPC review is an administrative check of required attribution and may take up to two weeks. Implement any requested acknowledgement, author-line, methods, or citation corrections before journal submission.
