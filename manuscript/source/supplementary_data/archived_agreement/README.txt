Original AMP-AD/CGGA descriptive agreement summary, independently checked from all 42 five-mask banks. The Nogueira normalization uses the observed union of five saved masks, matching the original audit implementation. It is not a coefficient computed on the entire pre-search Direct universe. TCGA coefficients reported elsewhere use their fixed full post-Direct universe; these normalizations should not be interchanged. No GA, classifier, score fitting or held-out evaluation was performed.
