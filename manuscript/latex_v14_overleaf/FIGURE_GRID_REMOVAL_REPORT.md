# V10 figure grid-removal and annotation audit

Date: 2026-08-06 (America/Los_Angeles)

## Scope and outcome

The original 23-figure V10 audit covered Main Figures 1--5 and Supplementary Figures S1--S18. V11 Supplementary Figure S19, the V12 private-radiomics Supplementary Figure S20, additive post-freeze Supplementary Figures S21--S23, and controlled-simulation Supplementary Figure S24 were subsequently checked under the same policy. Main Figure 1 required no plot-grid change because its lines are workflow structure; its a) and b) label fonts were later normalized to the same size without changing scientific content or geometry. Background plot grids were removed from quantitative panels. Heatmap cell boundaries, axes, error bars, data-connecting lines, panel separators, and scientifically defined reference lines (including zero lines, ROC diagonals, the 0.01 regret bound, and the 0.5 failure threshold) were retained.

Supplementary Figure S18 required the only axis-label repair. Panels a and b now explicitly label the shared row axis as `Center`, the categorical column axis as `Branch-cap condition`, and the color scales as `Absolute target deviation` and `All-zero generations`. Their comparison headings are `Original configuration` and `Updated configuration`, replacing the normative `Legacy`--`Recommended` pair. Panel c now uses explicit row-level y-axis labels (`Absolute target deviation` and `All-zero generations`), increased inter-row spacing, `Aggregated by center` and `Aggregated by GA branch` facet headings, and a shared `Original`--`Updated` legend outside the plotted data. These changes remove the label obstruction visible in the earlier layout without changing any plotted value. The other 22 figures already contained appropriate explicit, categorical, shared, or diagram-specific axis information; no additional axis text was added to them.

No figure, table, or manuscript item was renumbered. No empirical value, point estimate, interval, feature count, score, label, legend, or scientific conclusion was altered.

## Reproducibility

`analysis/rebuild_v10_gridfree_figures.py` retains the deterministic presentation history for the frozen V10 assets. V12 Figure 4 is derived from its preserved editable SVG by `scripts/build_gridfree_figure4_svg.py`, which asserts removal of exactly 63 grid groups and one empty annotation box; the corresponding headless render is byte-for-byte reproducible. V12 Figure 5 is generated natively by `scripts/build_cgga_figure5.py` from three manuscript-local aggregate CSVs; it uses a 170-mm canvas, equal-size panels, no background grid, and bold a)--c) labels as the only bold in-panel text. Supplementary Figure S9 is generated natively by `scripts/build_cgga_s9_clean.py` from three frozen manuscript-local aggregate CSVs. Supplementary Figure S18 is regenerated from its archived 24-condition CSV by the bundled builder under `supplementary_data/recommended_mode_120_run/scripts/`. Supplementary Figure S20 is generated natively by `scripts/build_radiomics_s20.py`. Supplementary Figures S21--S23 are generated by `scripts/build_postfreeze_results.py`; their grids are disabled at source, the builder rejects y-label intrusion into a preceding panel, and consecutive PDF/SVG/PNG builds are hash-identical.

This is presentation-only processing. It does not run GA, RFECV, Direct selection, held-out evaluation, Bayesian analysis, STABL, BLiP, bootstrap analysis, or any empirical feature-selection experiment.
